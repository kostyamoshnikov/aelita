Konstantin Moshnikov

Producer · Circus artist

Produces and promotes creative projects. For over 15 years a circus artist, performing in “Princess of the Circus” at the Saint Petersburg Theatre of Musical Comedy.

Knows the cultural industry from the inside: experience in organising film shoots, tours and staging performances. Works regularly with commercial and state venues — from the Alexandrinsky Theatre to Planetarium No. 1 and Skorokhod venues.

At AELITA PRODUCTION he's responsible for promotion, social media and organisational production. Organiser of the “Tochka Cuire” and BUFEST 2026.
