Tickets

Where can I buy tickets to a show?

There is a purchase link on the card of every performance in the Schedule and on the production page.

  • We sell some performances ourselves, on this website: sign in to your account with your email and pay for the order on the YooKassa payment page.
  • If a performance is held by a theatre, venue or festival, the button leads to their website: the ticket is sold by the organiser of the performance, under its own rules.
  • No purchase button means sales have not opened yet or are already closed. Not to miss the start, subscribe to our news.
  • Buy only via links from our website: a ticket from resellers or look-alike websites may turn out to be invalid.

How do I get my ticket, and do I need to print it?

The ticket is electronic: a PDF with a QR code arrives by email right after payment and is kept in your account under “My tickets”.

  • No need to print it — just show the QR code on your phone screen at the entrance.
  • Save the ticket to your phone in advance: there may be no signal at the venue.

The ticket email didn't arrive — what should I do?

Check your Spam and Promotions folders.

  • If you have an account with the same email you used to buy, press “Send tickets to my email” under “My tickets”.
  • Still nothing — write to aelita.production@yandex.ru with your order number and we will send it again.

Does a child need a ticket?

Yes — every visitor needs their own ticket, even a child who is held on a lap. Check the production’s age rating.

Can I pay with a Pushkin Card?

Yes, if the performance is marked “Pushkin Card” — on its card in the Schedule or on the production page.

  • Such tickets are sold by a venue that accepts the card — the purchase button leads to it. The Pushkin Card cannot be used to pay on our website.
  • No mark — check with the venue box office: for touring performances we do not always know this in advance.

How do I cancel a registration for a free event?

Use the link in the registration confirmation email, or go to your account, “My event registrations”. Your place will go to someone else.

Refunds and changes

How do I return a ticket and how much will I get back?

Submit a request on the ticket refund page: you will need the order code from the email and the email or phone used for the order. The amount depends on how many days before the performance the request is submitted:

  • 10 days or more — 100%; 5–9 days — 50%; 3–4 days — 30%; less than 3 days — no refund. The law (Article 52.1 of the Fundamentals of Russian Legislation on Culture) and the public offer, Section 6, apply.
  • Days are counted from the date of the request: a request on 21 October for a performance on 31 October is 10 days before.
  • If you are ill or a close relative has died, we refund the full price. Submit the request no later than the day of the performance; the document can be sent within 14 days after it.
  • The money goes back to the card you paid with within 10 days of the request. There is no exchange for another date: you can return the ticket and buy a new one.
  • Ticket bought on cuire-fest.ru — email aelita.production@yandex.ru with the order number and the reason. Bought at the venue box office or from another organiser — the refund is handled by the seller, under its rules.

Can’t go — can I give my ticket to someone else?

Yes, if the ticket was bought on our website: in your account, under “My tickets”, the “Transfer ticket” button.

  • A ticket can be transferred once and no later than 3 hours before the start. The new holder receives the ticket with a new QR code by email, and the previous QR code stops working.
  • No button — email aelita.production@yandex.ru, and we will help.

What happens if a show is postponed or cancelled?

We will notify you by the email and phone from the order and post the news in the Schedule.

  • If the show is cancelled, we refund the full price to the same card ourselves — no application needed.
  • If it is postponed, the ticket is valid for the new date. If the date does not suit you, we refund the full price on request.
  • If the ticket was bought from a theatre, venue or festival, the seller will notify you of changes and refund the money.

Tickets got cheaper or the performance became free — will you refund the difference?

No: the ticket price is fixed at the moment of payment (public offer, clause 4.5).

  • Discounts, promotions, invitation-only and free performances of the same production announced later do not change the price of a ticket already bought.
  • But if the performance is cancelled, replaced or postponed, you are entitled to a full refund.

At the performance

Can I come in after the show has started?

Once the show starts, the hall is closed.

  • Latecomers are admitted during the interval. If there is no interval, entry is no longer possible, and the ticket price is not refunded in that case.
  • Entry to the venue usually opens no earlier than an hour before the start. If a performance has a different procedure, it is stated on its page.

What do the age ratings 12+, 16+, 18+ mean?

This is the age rating under the law on protecting children from harmful information (No. 436-FZ). It is shown on every poster and production page. For 18+ performances, you may be asked for an ID confirming your age at the entrance.

What is not allowed at a show?

Taking photos, filming or recording audio is not allowed without our permission — the shows are protected by copyright.

  • You may not bring food or drinks into the hall, come with animals (except guide dogs) or come intoxicated.
  • Our productions may use strobe lights, loud sound, stage smoke and actors working very close to the audience — if this matters to you, check the description on the show page.
  • The full rules are in the visiting rules.

Is there a version of the website for the visually impaired, and help for visitors with disabilities?

Yes. The eye icon in the site header (or the “Version for the visually impaired” link in the menu and at the bottom of the page) turns on large text, high-contrast colours and a no-images mode.

  • If you use a wheelchair or need help at the entrance, email aelita.production@yandex.ru or call +7 960 515-16-90 in advance: we will tell you how the entrance to the venue works and help with a seat in the hall.
  • A companion and a guide dog are admitted under the law and the venue’s rules.

Subscription and gifts

How do I use a gift card?

Enter the code from the certificate when buying tickets on our website, in the “I have a gift card” field.

  • You don’t have to spend the whole amount at once: the balance stays on the card.
  • You can check the balance on the gift card page.

How does the AELITA COMMUNITY subscription work?

You subscribe on the AELITA COMMUNITY page, after signing in to your account. The first payment is taken at once, then the money is charged automatically once a month, on the same day.

  • The main stream is 5,000 ₽ a month; the entrepreneurs’ stream is 10,000 ₽ a month.
  • An email arrives 3 days before each charge, and a fiscal receipt after it.
  • If a charge fails, we retry once a day, up to three times; after the third failure the subscription is paused.
  • Card details are stored by the payment service YooKassa — we keep only the card type, the last four digits and the expiry date. Terms: public offer, section 14.

How do I cancel the subscription?

At any time — in your account, “Subscriptions” section, “Cancel subscription” button, or via the link in the email about the upcoming charge.

  • After cancelling, no more money is charged, and your participation continues until the end of the paid month.
  • Changed your mind — the same section has a “Resume subscription” button.

Can I get a refund for the subscription?

Yes. Within 7 days of a payment we refund the full amount; after that, the cost of the unused days of the current month.

  • The “Cancel and refund” button is in your account, “Subscriptions” section; you can also write to aelita.production@yandex.ru. The button shows the amount in advance.
  • Participation ends on the day of the request. If the month was paid with a gift card, only the amount paid in money is refunded.

News and contact

How do I find out about new dates and premieres?

Leave your email in the form on the Schedule page or write to our Telegram bot. Announcements also go out on social media: VKontakte, Telegram, YouTube, Dzen.

Can AELITA PRODUCTION bring a show to my city?

Yes, we are open to touring and working with venues. Tell us about your venue in the Collaborate section — we will reply and discuss the details.

Didn’t find an answer? Write to us — we will reply within a few days.
