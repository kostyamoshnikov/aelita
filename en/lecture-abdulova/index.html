Art as a Startup: Engineering Attention and Reality

Katya Abdulova
Curator and producer of the "Tochka Cuire" contemporary art festival

About the talk

Business is used to defending itself against uncertainty with strategy sessions and plans — the artist doesn't fight uncertainty, but plays with it, and always wins: it's artists who create the image of the future and the reality each of us carries in our heads. Launching the festival in Stary Oskol was the same kind of startup: zero budget, no cultural habit among the audience, and total unknown.

The lecture covers how directing tools, systems thinking and creative practice turn a point that doesn't exist on any map into a place of attraction for thousands of people; why contemporary art is really about the hard work of building communities; and how founders can stop searching for a magic "growth" button by learning to create meaning where others see only industrial pipes.

Nobody wanted an iPhone until Steve Jobs invented it.
