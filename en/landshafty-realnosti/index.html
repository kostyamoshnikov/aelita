Millions of years ago we were given life. Now you know what to do with it.

An open conversation with Katya Abdulova — a film talk on Luc Besson’s “Lucy”. The story of a heroine who suddenly gains abilities she never knew she had is a starting point for talking about how we see reality and what changes when the very rules by which we perceive it change.

Host

Katya Abdulova — curator of cultural projects, researcher, producer, psychologist by training (Higher School of Psychology). Co-founder of AELITA PRODUCTION, curator and producer of the “Tochka Cuire” festival.
