About AELITA PRODUCTION

  • AELITA PRODUCTION is a theatre production company, founded in 2025 by Ekaterina Abdulova and Konstantin Moshnikov.
  • In under a year of work — more than 10 completed projects, presence in 4 Russian cities.
  • The company produces the creation, distribution and touring of productions at the intersection of contemporary theatre, choreography and visual art.
  • On October 22–25, 2026, at TsSI “Byl” (Stary Oskol), the company is holding the first edition of the “Tochka Cuire” contemporary art festival.
  • Legal entity: LLC “AELITA PRODUCTION,” TIN 4000027320, PSRN 1254000006644.
