How it works

Not a recommendation engine. Personal curation

Some services suggest "what similar users liked." And then there's a person who reads your questionnaire and thinks about you specifically. Katya is the second type. She calls herself an architect, not a recommendation algorithm.

You fill in a questionnaire — about yourself, what's happening in your life right now, which genres and themes resonate, what's missing. Katya reads it personally, with no intermediaries and no automation, and puts together a selection of five books — with a note for each one, explaining why this particular book, and why now.

You truly exist only where you think, feel, experience, want and act differently. There is no such thing as "everyone".

We buy the physical books ourselves and send them to you. A personal letter from Katya comes with each book: why this particular book, and why now. Not a link to a marketplace — a parcel with the books and a letter inside.

For over 13 years Katya has been reading and choosing books. Here it's the same skill — but not for a group, personally for you.
