About the project

A physical theatre piece in which lightness and weight, tradition and modernity, reality and metaphor interweave.

The costumes for the show were created by the Kozelsk brand “Molodo-Zeleno” (House of Pavlov) — hand embroidery and pearl ornaments in the Russian style.
