About the production

Ivan Ivanovich vs Ivan Nikiforovich. Two former friends made a drama out of nothing.

Gogol is not just the author — he is the chief arbitrator in this tragicomic battle. He is on stage. He sees everything.

And he knows why this story keeps repeating itself again and again.
