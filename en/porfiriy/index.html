About the production

The murder of the old moneylender. No evidence. Motive unknown. The suspects have scattered through a bamboo grove, mingling with young officers.

Meditation. Ikigai. Bushido. Only this will help Porfiry solve the most sinister crime in Russian literature.
GenreMeditative detective piece
Age rating16+
DirectorAlexander Panov
Source materialDostoevsky, Akutagawa
