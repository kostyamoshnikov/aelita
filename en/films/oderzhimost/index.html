Film · 2014

Whiplash

Damien Chazelle’s drama about a young jazz drummer and the teacher who pushes him to the limit.

The story

Andrew Neiman joins an elite music school determined to make it as a jazz drummer. Working with bandleader Terence Fletcher, he starts to question how far he is willing to go to earn a place in the band.

Written and directed by Damien Chazelle. With Miles Teller and J. K. Simmons. About 106 minutes.

Questions for the film

  • When does a teacher’s insistence help a student, and when does it take away their freedom?
  • What do we hear about power and control in the rhythm, tempo and pauses?
  • What does each character think makes a musician great?

The film at Sundance

In 2014, Whiplash won both the U.S. Dramatic Grand Jury Prize and the U.S. Dramatic Audience Award.
