This Agreement governs the use of cookies and similar technologies on the website https://aelita-production.ru (hereinafter — the “Website”) of LLC “AELITA PRODUCTION” (hereinafter — the “Company”, the “Operator”).

1. General Provisions

1.1. Cookies are small text files stored on the user's device when visiting the website.

1.2. Necessary technologies support the requested Site functions. Yandex Metrica with Webvisor starts only through Accept in the separate cookie banner. Necessary only saves an analytics refusal without restricting purchases or the account. Browsing, email confirmation, purchasing and newsletter consent do not replace analytics consent.

2. Types of Cookies Used

Strictly necessary (technical) — ensure the basic operation of the Website: navigation, security, form state preservation. Disabling them may impair Website functionality.

Functional — remember your preferences (language, completed forms) for convenience.

Analytics — help analyse traffic and user behaviour (Yandex Metrica). Metrica runs with Session Replay (“Webvisor”) enabled: it records on-page activity — cursor movement, scrolling, clicks and navigation between pages — and allows a visit to be replayed later. The contents of form fields are not saved in these recordings, but the fact and sequence of actions are, so we do not treat such recordings as fully anonymised.

3. Processing Purposes

Ensuring the technical operation and security of the Site; improving usability; analysing audience and statistics, including recording of on-page activity via Session Replay (“Webvisor”, see clause 2); content personalisation.

4. Retention Period

Cookie lifetimes range from a session to 1–2 years. The analytics choice, date and Agreement edition are stored in the browser for up to 180 days. Expiry or an edition change requires a new choice; analytics stays off until then. Data can be deleted in browser settings.

4.1. Browser storage

LocalStorage and sessionStorage hold the cookie choice, account session until sign-out, accessibility settings, purchase/application drafts and an unsent review, dismissed hints and a temporary event-list copy. Drafts are deleted after successful submission/payment. Entered data is sent to the Company for the corresponding user action; local data can be deleted in browser settings.

5. Third-party Cookies

Yandex Metrica (operator: Yandex LLC) with Webvisor runs only after consent. The Company does not directly manage third-party storage. Stop analytics through Cookie preferences → Necessary only; previously saved third-party data can also be deleted in browser settings.

6. Management and Disabling

Cookie preferences are in the footer, or at the bottom of checkout. Withdrawing consent reloads the page to stop the loaded counter. Blocking all functional browser storage may restrict Site functions; declining analytics alone does not.

7. Amendments to the Policy

The Company publishes a new Agreement edition with its date; previous editions are on the Documents page. Consent is given only through Accept; browsing does not replace it. Withdraw through Cookie preferences → Necessary only. This stops subsequent analytics but does not erase information previously collected lawfully by its provider.
