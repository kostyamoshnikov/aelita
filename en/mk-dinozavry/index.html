Children’s programme · “Tochka Cuire”

25 October · 12:00–13:00 · 60 minutes · ages 4–8 · 15 places

“Dinosaurs in the City”

A workshop by the “Rechezavriya” studio of interactive child development and speech therapy

RegisterThe full children’s programme

About the session

The “Rechezavriya” studio of interactive child development and speech therapy invites you to the workshop “Dinosaurs in the City”! This is not just a workshop — it is a real dopamine hit against autumn boredom.

At the workshop, your child will

  • Learn the basics of design and make a stylish new friend.
  • Develop imagination and creative thinking with neuro-exercises.
  • Practise counting in Russian and English.

The hosts guarantee an individual approach to every child.

When and where

25 October, Sunday · 12:00–13:00 · CSI “Byl”, Stary Oskol, Zhukova microdistrict, 38.

For parents

A parent must be present at the session. The parent does not need a separate registration — one registration per child.

How to attend

Free, registration required

Participation is free; registration is required — places are limited. The art quest runs at the same time as the workshops — choose activities that don’t overlap.

If the button does not open the registration window — open the registration page

Getting there — CSI “Byl”, Stary Oskol, Zhukova microdistrict 38. Questions — aelita.production@yandex.ru or +7 960 515-16-90.

Hosts

“Rechezavriya”

The city’s only studio of interactive child development and speech therapy: new-generation development technologies for children from 1 to 10. It does not replace proven teaching methods with technology but brings together the specialist’s work, method, interactive tools (an interactive floor, a smart board, a magic sandbox, a magic mirror), movement, play and emotion into one developmental environment. More than 15 areas: speech therapist, neuropsychologist, mini-kindergarten, early development, school preparation, English, drawing and modelling, chess, attention school, olympiad problems, calligraphy, speed reading, creative workshops.
