OMYT Dance Space

Choreographer and artistic director — Nadezhda Kokhanova

Four works at “Tochka Cuire”

OMYT (pronounced “Omut”, Russian for “whirlpool”) is a contemporary dance space in Saint Petersburg: a school for adults and children and a dance company. Classes run from complete beginners to advanced level, and the company premieres its work on the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre.

Founder and lead choreographer — Nadezhda Kokhanova, director and teacher. Her pieces are made in a live process together with specific people — which is why they feel close, clear and resonant to the audience. Nadezhda often performs on stage alongside her dancers.

The space's mission is to make dance a familiar and natural part of life.

“Prophecies of Migratory Birds”

Site-specific performance for the 200th anniversary of Alexandria Park, Peterhof

At the “Tochka Cuire” festival

Four OMYT works over three days — CSI “Byl”, Stary Oskol. Producer — Mikhail Kazmiruk, curator of the “New Dance” programme at the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre.

23 October, 19:00 · Red Hall

Prophecies of Migratory Birds

Site-specific performance: eight performers in costumes of the birds that return to Alexandria Park every summer.

 24 October, 18:00 · Blue Hall

The Rashomon Effect

A production reflecting on the nature of human vice — through the imagery of Akutagawa and Kurosawa.

 25 October, 13:00 · Gallery

Mayday

A physical-theatre reflection: the distress signal is sent — but the disaster never comes.

 25 October, 19:00 · Blue Hall

The Scent of Rose Oil

A physical-theatre sketch on the biblical strand of “The Master and Margarita” — about choice and its price.
