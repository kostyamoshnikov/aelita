Children’s programme · “Tochka Cuire”

24 October · 13:00–14:00 · 60 minutes · 8+ · 15 places

A natural beeswax candle

A workshop by the “VmesteArt” creative space

RegisterThe full children’s programme

About the session

A fascinating and fragrant creative session. Young makers will learn interesting facts about bees and the nature of wax, and then roll a real natural candle from beeswax sheets with their own hands. The process is safe and eco-friendly, develops fine motor skills and patience, and brings plenty of joy.

When and where

24 October, Saturday · 13:00–14:00 · CSI “Byl”, Stary Oskol, Zhukova microdistrict, 38.

What the child takes home

A finished scented candle made with their own hands, to warm the home and delight the whole family.

For parents

Children under 8 take part together with a parent. A parent must be present at the start and the end of the workshop — for older children too.

How to attend

Free, registration required

Participation is free; registration is required — places are limited. The art quest runs at the same time as the workshops — choose activities that don’t overlap.

Hosts

“VmesteArt”

A creative space in Stary Oskol where creativity is open to everyone. Workshops for children and adults — in the studio and on the road, atmospheric art hen parties, and space to rent for warm-hearted events. Its mission is to bring people together through art and help them unlock their creative potential; the team pays close attention to the quality of materials and to safety.
