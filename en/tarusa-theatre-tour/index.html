AELITA PRODUCTION presents

Tarusa.
A theatre journey

A cultural day out for your team

A day in Tarusa: a theatrical walk through the town, a visit to a historic space, an intimate performance and tea together.

Discuss your journeyThe day’s programme ↓
1 / 10

Tarusa holds the stories of artists, writers and musicians. Discover the town through their stories on a team day that brings walking, theatre and conversation together in one journey.

01 / Programme

How the day unfolds

This is an indicative schedule. Departure and return times, transfers and the performance duration depend on your starting city, venue and chosen production.

  1. 11:00–12:00

    Departure for Tarusa

    An organised transfer from your chosen city, with group accompaniment.

  2. 12:00–14:00

    Theatrical Tarusa

    A walk through the historic centre with theatrical episodes, literary stories and encounters with characters.

    The route explores the town’s streets and architecture, literary and artistic places, and the stories of residents and visitors. Stops and theatrical episodes are selected for each programme.

  3. 14:00–15:00

    Transfer and introduction to the venue

    Arrival, time to explore the space and a break before the performance.

  4. 15:00–16:30

    An AELITA PRODUCTION performance

    An intimate performance in a historic or cultural space. Its architecture and atmosphere become part of the audience’s experience.

    The production is chosen for your group, the occasion and the venue’s possibilities.

    View the performances ↓
  5. 16:30–17:30

    Tea together

    Tea, refreshments, conversation, photographs and free time.

    Depending on the programme, options may include a tea ceremony, local produce and a short musical programme.

  6. 17:30–19:00

    Return journey

    An organised transfer back to the departure point.

02 / Spaces

Possible venues

The chosen space shapes the atmosphere of the journey and the conditions for the performance.

The Verzilins’ House

An intimate performance bringing actors and audience close together, with theatrical episodes connected to the place’s history.

Istomino Estate

A cultural day in the countryside: theatre, tea and a walk around the grounds.

Moscow Union of Artists — MSKh Tarusa

A performance in an artistic setting, an introduction to the space and an additional cultural programme.

House of Writers

A literary evening, solo performance, sound drama or another intimate theatrical format.

Availability, capacity, technical conditions, tea arrangements and use of the grounds require prior agreement with each venue’s owners or management.

05 / Organisation

One organiser for the whole day

AELITA PRODUCTION develops the programme and coordinates the event.

  • The concept and journey’s script
  • Performance selection and venue coordination
  • The theatrical walk
  • Artists, technical crew and contractors
  • Tea and transfers
  • Event accompaniment

One organiser is responsible for the agreed programme and coordination of everyone involved.

06 / Participants

A shared cultural experience

For companies looking to spend time together with employees, partners or clients.

The format suits business owners and management teams, corporate clubs and private communities, HR departments, marketing and PR teams. It can also form part of a VIP client loyalty programme, partner meeting or client event.
