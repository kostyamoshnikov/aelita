“Tochka Cuire” · 24–25 October

24 and 25 October · 12:00–16:00 · ages 4+ · free

Kids' programme

ScheduleFestival programme

Two days for children

Two days of children’s programme at the “Tochka Cuire” contemporary art festival at CSI “Byl”: an art quest in four sessions and seven activities for children aged 4 and up (the quest is 6+). All activities are free.

How to sign up

Every activity requires registration; workshops have 15 to 20 places. Registration is open for all activities: the “Register” button is on each activity’s page and in the “How to attend” section below. The art quest runs at the same time as the workshops — choose sessions that don’t overlap. If a parent takes part with their child, the activity page says whether they need a separate registration.

Programme partners

“VmesteArt”

A creative space in Stary Oskol where creativity is open to everyone. Workshops for children and adults — in the studio and on the road, atmospheric art hen parties, and space to rent for warm-hearted events. Its mission is to bring people together through art and help them unlock their creative potential; the team pays close attention to the quality of materials and to safety.

Komsomolsky Prospekt, 73A · +7 (918) 459-35-45

vk.ru/vmesteart31

“Academy of Clever Kids”

A centre that trains the intellect of children and adults through play: speed reading, the Rubik’s cube, puzzles and memory training, calligraphy, sessions with a speech therapist and a psychologist. Its head is Yana Barabanova, an educational psychologist and speech therapist with 27 years of experience and a mother of many children.

Zvyozdny microdistrict, 1 (entrance from the courtyard) · 8 904 093-22-93

vk.ru/akademiyaumnikov

Yulia Candle

Yulia’s cosy studio: more than 10 kinds of workshops (plaster, candles and more) for children’s parties, hen parties and corporate events — she brings the script and the materials, and participants leave with a finished piece. Ready-made things too: candles, scented sachets, wax melts, diffusers, vases and candy dishes.

vk.ru/juliacandle1997

“Rechezavriya”

The city’s only studio of interactive child development and speech therapy: new-generation development technologies for children from 1 to 10. It does not replace proven teaching methods with technology but brings together the specialist’s work, method, interactive tools (an interactive floor, a smart board, a magic sandbox, a magic mirror), movement, play and emotion into one developmental environment. More than 15 areas: speech therapist, neuropsychologist, mini-kindergarten, early development, school preparation, English, drawing and modelling, chess, attention school, olympiad problems, calligraphy, speed reading, creative workshops.

Stepnoy microdistrict, 22 · Gubkina Ave., 5 · +7 980 083-69-85

vk.ru/rechezavriya

“Myod” party studio

A cosy venue in the city centre with panoramic windows: themed photo zones, favourite characters to choose from, lights and music, photography and shows.

Olimpiysky, 63, “Oskol” shopping centre, 2nd floor

vk.ru/stydia_med
