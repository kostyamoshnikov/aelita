About the production

"Pushkin. The Shot. The Blizzard." is a production from the "Extracurricular Classics" cycle, created to bring classical art closer to young people and school students. A stage adaptation of two Alexander Pushkin novellas — "The Shot" and "The Blizzard" — performed by actors of the Kaluga Regional Drama Theatre.

The audience will see two stories — of courage and composure, of irony of fate and the ability to wait, of love and honour, of a thirst for revenge and generosity. The production is accompanied by live classical music — works by Johannes Brahms and Astor Piazzolla are performed on stage.
