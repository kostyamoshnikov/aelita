Talk · lecture series opening

How dance is born and lives in the real world

Mikhail Kazmiruk
Curator of the “New Dance” programme at the Alexandrinsky Theatre's New Stage

In three years, the “New Dance” programme has produced more than 150 projects — among them the show “A Place That Didn't Exist Yet”, a Golden Mask laureate, and the Saint Petersburg premiere of Ohad Naharin's “Choir”.

Behind the artistic instinct is a rare technical background: as technical director of the imarussia agency he builds hybrid conferences and ceremonies for major corporations — SGK, “Vkusno — i tochka”, IBS — and as producer of the ODYSSEY festival he runs four stages for 10,000 spectators.

A “symbiosis of director and event engineer” — on the bridges between the poetry of movement and hard logistics, between an intimate performance and a large-scale open-air.

Artificial intelligence: what it can already do, and where it gets it wrong

Alexey Tsymlov
CEO and founder of the IT company Syntropic

Machines have learned to write, draw and talk — their own Curie point has already happened here too. No hype: what neural networks do today, where they get it wrong, and how they're already used in theatre, design and production.

With live examples and audience Q&A — from how AI assistants work to what changes in a profession once a machine takes over part of the job.

Workshop

How to sound like someone people want to trust

Alena Bychkova
Journalist, host of “Morning of Russia” on Russia-1

Her career began at REN TV and has continued at Russia-1 for more than 17 years. A born moderator and dialogue producer: she hosts “Open Studio” radio broadcasts, moderates the “Berega” media forum and spoken-word competitions, and her author interviews range from politics to culture.

How to remove the metal and tension from your speech. Where to find the calm, low notes that put a listener at ease. How simple pauses bring you closer to an audience than the loudest words ever could. And why perfect diction can sometimes sound less honest than a little roughness.

Your voice is an invitation to dialogue.

Talk

Art as a Startup: Engineering Attention and Reality

Katya Abdulova
Curator and producer of the “Tochka Cuire” contemporary art festival

Business is used to defending itself against uncertainty with strategy sessions and plans — the artist doesn't fight uncertainty, but plays with it, and always wins: it's artists who create the image of the future and the reality each of us carries in our heads. Launching the festival in Stary Oskol was the same kind of startup: zero budget, no cultural habit among the audience, and total unknown.

The lecture covers how directing tools, systems thinking and creative practice turn a point that doesn't exist on any map into a place of attraction for thousands of people; why contemporary art is really about the hard work of building communities; and how founders can stop searching for a magic “growth” button by learning to create meaning where others see only industrial pipes.

Nobody wanted an iPhone until Steve Jobs invented it.
