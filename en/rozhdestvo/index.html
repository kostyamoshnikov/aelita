One story in seven chapters

From the first welcome to personal notes, with selected scenes from Frank Capra’s It’s a Wonderful Life.

  1. IWe were expecting you

    A welcome drink, introductions and the first signs of the story.

  2. IIDreams that might have been

    Film and a conversation about what makes a life feel fulfilled.

  3. IIIWords that stay with us

    Christmas post, thanks and personal notes.

  4. IVThe unseen mark

    Stories about the quiet difference we make in each other’s lives.

  5. VThe effect of being there

    A collective work made by the guests.

  6. VIA seat at the table

    Christmas refreshments, conversation, portraits and small surprises.

  7. VIII’m glad you’re here

    A closing message and a gift for each guest.
