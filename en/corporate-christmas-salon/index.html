The evening

Frank Capra’s It’s a Wonderful Life (1946) inspires the programme. What might the world be like if we had never been part of it?

  1. Christmas postEach guest receives an envelope with a question we’ll return to at the end.
  2. Film and philosophyWe discuss George Bailey’s story, his choices and the people whose lives he touched.
  3. Around the Christmas tableTea and refreshments at a shared table.
  4. The World Without MeReturn to the question in your envelope. Write your answer, keep it to yourself or share it with the group.
  5. Letters and postcardsWrite to someone close to you or leave a wish for another guest.
