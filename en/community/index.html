Book club

Why “LEVEL 3”

A book is only a pretext. A pretext to run into someone else’s system of coordinates inside your own head and, for a few hours, allow at least one “uncomfortable thought”.

Level 1

You read it, got the plot, agreed or argued — and put it back on the shelf.

Level 2

You see ideas, context, connections; you compare them with your own life, take some things on and reject others.

Level 3

You stop taking the book apart sentence by sentence and start taking apart the way you take it apart.

We read books. But we actually gather here for something else: to see what we build our own world from.

— Katya Abdulova, founder of the club

FAQ

How does payment work?

The subscription is monthly: you pay for the first month when you sign up, then the same amount is charged to your card automatically on the same day every month until you cancel. We email you 3 days before every payment. You can cancel at any time — in your account or via the link in that email.

How do I cancel?

In your personal account, with the “Cancel subscription” button. You stay in until the end of the paid month; if you change your mind, you can resume it there too.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Within 7 days of a payment we refund the full amount; later — the cost of the unused days of the month. The “Cancel and refund” button is in your personal account; participation ends on the day of the refund.

Can I join online?

Yes, by video call during the meeting itself. Every meeting is recorded — you can watch at your own pace.

What happens after payment?

You will get an email with a confirmation and a receipt. Within a day the organiser will contact you and add you to the stream chat — the meeting schedule and the books are there.

Do I need to prepare for meetings?

Yes: members read the book, take notes and bring their own questions — that is how the conversation is born. There will be no ready-made answers or lectures.

Do I need a personal account?

Yes, the subscription is linked to your personal account — it shows until when you have paid, and that is where you cancel or get a refund. You can create it right on the payment page.

Can a company pay?

Payment by invoice from an organisation is being set up. For now, email us at aelita.production@yandex.ru — we will help.
