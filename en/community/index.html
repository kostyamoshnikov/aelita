AELITA COMMUNITY

The AELITA PRODUCTION community

Spaces for people who aren't looking for a ready-made answer, but want to understand how thinking works — their own and other people's. Right now AELITA COMMUNITY has two reading streams; other formats — not necessarily about books — will follow over time.

Community

More than one format

AELITA COMMUNITY is a shared home for formats that don't reduce to ready-made answers. For now it holds one active project — the book club; over time other directions may join it, not necessarily about reading.

What they'll share is this: not a lecture with ready conclusions, but an environment where the friction between different ways of thinking is itself the mechanism that moves understanding forward.
