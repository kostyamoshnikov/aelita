About company “Koroche”

Yulia Bychkova founded the company in 2020. The repertoire already features more than five titles — all productions in the contemporary genre.

The young, dynamically developing company went on its first tour to Saint Petersburg. “Stories. Of the Earthly and the Heavenly” was successfully performed at the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre.
