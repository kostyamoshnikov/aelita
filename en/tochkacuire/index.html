About the festival

An annual festival in the heart of an industrial city

AELITA PRODUCTION — a production company “where the theatre of the future is born”. We bring our entire repertoire to Stary Oskol — contemporary dance, physical theatre, sound drama and postmodernist readings of classics — and unfold it on one stage over four days.

“TOCHKA CUIRE” is conceived as an annual event at CSI “Byl”, bringing together a contemporary arts audience in Stary Oskol year after year. 2026 is the first edition, where we set the tradition.

01 · TRANSITION

Transition

Art as a point of no return — the audience, the space and the city are never quite the same afterwards.

02 · ENERGY

Energy

Heat, movement, performativity. The festival's formats are charged with physical and theatrical energy.

03 · ATTRACTION

Attraction

The magnetic nature of the point: the festival brings together the diverse — genres, generations, classics and the contemporary — into one.

~770°C

The Curie point of iron — the temperature at which metal loses and regains its magnetic properties. Discovered by Pierre Cuire in 1895.

The name speaks directly to the city's identity: Stary Oskol sits on the Kursk Magnetic Anomaly — the world's largest deposit of magnetic iron ore. The Curie point is the moment when a substance changes state. This is what the festival does.

Fashion show

“Molodo-Zeleno” runway show

The festival programme includes a runway show by the Kozelsk brand “Molodo-Zeleno” (House of Pavlov) — tradition and modernity, hand embroidery and pearl ornaments in the Russian style. Founder of the brand — Tatiana Pavlova; collection designer — her daughter, Maria Pavlova.

The brand has already collaborated with AELITA PRODUCTION — “Molodo-Zeleno” created the costumes for the show “Stories. Of the Earthly and the Heavenly”.

Kids' programme

Art quest “Kids Aren't Spectators”

The contemporary art festival has been taken over by “Grey Screens” — entities that steal colour and emotion. Kids aren't spectators here, but a squad of superheroes ready for a rescue mission: they have just 60 minutes to reboot the art objects and bring back the joy.

It's a quest story where the child becomes a co-author of the art objects. Collect clues, get paint on your hands, use your imagination, and take control of the exhibition — the quest turns passive contemplation into active creation. The child is the programme's lead artist.

Tired of art being “quiet and proper”? At a contemporary art festival, we allow noise. Take a breather for yourself and give your child a celebration — we'll keep the kids busy with art while you soak up the culture.
