Children’s programme · “Tochka Cuire”

24 and 25 October · 12:30 and 14:00 · 60 minutes · 0+

Art quest “Kids Aren't Spectators”

RegisterFestival programme

About the quest

The contemporary art festival has been taken over by “Grey Screens” — entities that steal colour and emotion. Kids aren't spectators here, but a squad of superheroes ready for a rescue mission: they have just 60 minutes to reboot the art objects and bring back the joy.

How it works

It's a quest story where the child becomes a co-author of the art objects. Collect clues, get paint on your hands, use your imagination, and take control of the exhibition — the quest turns passive contemplation into active creation. The child is the programme's lead artist.

For adults

Take a breather for yourself and make it a treat for your child: while the kids are busy with art, adults can dive into the rest of the festival programme. On Saturday from 13:00 to 16:00 there are workshops for children and adults, and a market in the foyer all day.

Tired of art being “quiet and proper”? At a contemporary art festival, we allow noise.

How to attend

Free, registration required

Four sessions — Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October, at 12:30 and at 14:00. Participation is free, 0+, places are limited; the quest lasts 60 minutes, come for the start. Registration required: choose the day and time in the form.
