AELITA PRODUCTION — a theatre production company. We find bright talents and bold ideas, and produce, distribute and tour performances across Russia.

We work at the intersection of contemporary theatre, choreography and visual art. We create projects after which it is impossible to see the world the same way.

History

2025

Company founded

Ekaterina Abdulova and Konstantin Moshnikov founded AELITA PRODUCTION. First projects — working with dance companies and directors creating contemporary theatre outside the mainstream.

2025

First productions

Production “Stories. Of the Earthly and the Heavenly”, “Listen!” and “A Guide for Novice Wanderers”. First tour. Innovative Cultural Centre, Kaluga.

2026

Flagship and expansion

Flagship production release “IOV: Material” — Ekaterina Abdulova's directorial debut. Tour to Saint Petersburg, performances at the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre.

2026

Festival direction

Festival launch “Tochka Cuire” (October, Stary Oskol). Expansion to new venues — Tarusa, Stary Oskol.

How we work

01

We find talent

We work with directors, choreographers and artists who create art with their own perspective. We don't replicate — we open new voices.

02

We produce

From idea to performance — we handle organisation, financing, marketing and logistics. The artist focuses on art, we handle everything else.

03

We tour the regions

Touring is our priority. Contemporary theatre should be accessible beyond the capitals. We build routes across Russia.
