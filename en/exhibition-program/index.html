“Tochka Cuire” · Exhibition Programme · Season I

Transition

How to get there · free entry

Twenty-eight artists — not neighbours in a catalogue, but one route: the visitor walks a path from the entry point to the exit point, and it is one path, not a list of stops.

October 14–25, 2026
CSI “Byl”, Stary Oskol
Free admission
← Back to “Tochka Cuire”

Transition is the principle behind the whole composition of the exhibition. We gathered a pool of twenty-eight artists — their works here do not sit side by side like exhibits in a catalogue — they are arranged in a single line: the visitor walks a path from the entry point to the exit point, and it is one path, not a list of stops.

The titles of the works form this road on their own — simply list them one after another:

birthday→before it became clear→transition→observer→growing in→initiation→dream or waking→rebirth→catharsis→liberation→it's good where you are

Each artist named their own work this way, without knowing the other twenty-seven. Yet together these titles read as a single phrase — from threshold to awakening, from dissolving to contact, from contact to what remains after.

That is “Transition”: the artists do not illustrate — they produce a reality, a state the visitor passes through. One and the same threshold — birth, rupture, recognition, liberation — is shown here from twenty-eight different points at once.

The visitor does not choose which work to look at: they walk through them as through a single route, and end up somewhere other than where they started.
