Business Programme · "Tochka Cuire"

October 23 · 18:00 · Red Hall

How dance is born and lives in the real world

Mikhail Kazmiruk
Curator of the “New Dance” programme at the Alexandrinsky Theatre's New Stage
For the press

About the talk

In three years, the “New Dance” programme has produced more than 150 projects — among them the show “A Place That Didn't Exist Yet”, a Golden Mask laureate, and the Saint Petersburg premiere of Ohad Naharin's “Choir”.

Behind the artistic instinct is a rare technical background: as technical director of the imarussia agency he builds hybrid conferences and ceremonies for major corporations — SGK, “Vkusno — i tochka”, IBS — and as producer of the ODYSSEY festival he runs four stages for 10,000 spectators.

A “symbiosis of director and event engineer” — on the bridges between the poetry of movement and hard logistics, between an intimate performance and a large-scale open-air.
