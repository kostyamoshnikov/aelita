About the show

“Dyba Show” is a solo stand-up concert by Vanya Dyba, an AELITA PRODUCTION resident. Part of the comedy is born right on stage, in dialogue with the audience, part is time-tested material of his own.

Format

Improvisation and his own tested material

Themes

Intellectual and philosophical — in plain-spoken Russian

Running time

90 minutes · 18+

When and where

October 25, 20:00 · CSI “Byl”, Red Hall

In the “Tochka Cuire” festival programme

The concert is part of “Tochka Cuire” — the AELITA contemporary art festival at CSI “Byl” (22–25 October): dance, physical theatre, sound drama, exhibitions. “Dyba Show” closes the festival on its final night.

Who is Vanya Dyba

A Russian stand-up comedian, on stage since 2015 — when he joined the creative collective “Zhivoy Ugolok.”

By training — a social worker, journalist and master’s graduate in education. By calling — an entrepreneur and creative consultant: head of LIFT TV and the TOLK music label, co-owner of the restaurant “Patio Pasta & Cocktails.” Married, raising a son.

Host of the “Vinogradny Podcast” — guests have included Alexey Shcherbakov, Sasha Maloy, Zhenya Sidorov, Boris Zeliger, Vasya Medvedev and Nastya Venevitina.
