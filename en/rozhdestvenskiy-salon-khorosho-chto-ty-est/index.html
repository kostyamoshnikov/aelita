Christmas philosophy salon

I’m Glad You Exist

An evening about what it means to be human, with Frank Capra’s 1946 film It’s a Wonderful Life.

Date, venue and booking details to be announced

Approx. 3 hours 45 minutes

The evening

What would the world be like if you had never existed? The film sets that question in motion. We’ll pick it up through Christmas stories and Merab Mamardashvili’s philosophy, considering how love, attention and responsibility shape a human life.

Programme

  1. Christmas postPick up an envelope with a question to return to later.
  2. Becoming humanA short introduction to the Christmas story and Mamardashvili’s philosophical perspective.
  3. Film screeningWatch Frank Capra’s It’s a Wonderful Life and notice what changes in how George sees his life.
  4. Around the Christmas tableTea, something to eat and a chance to sit with the film before the conversation.
  5. The World Without MeReturn to your envelope and write, think or share with the group if you wish.
  6. Christmas lettersA closing thought over a postcard. You can write a letter to someone you’d like to thank or leave an anonymous wish for another guest.

The World Without Me

  • If I had never existed, what would be different in the world?
  • Who came into my life because I exist?
  • What could I do today to change someone’s life a little?

You’re welcome to keep your answers to yourself, skip any question or take part by listening.
