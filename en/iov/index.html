About the production

Directorial debut by Ekaterina Abdulova inspired by the biblical Book of Job, the philosophy of Kierkegaard and Mamardashvili, the prose of Kafka, Dostoevsky, Orwell and Chekhov.

This is a story of second birth. Of how, having lost everything, you stop being yourself.

Of how, at the edge of fear, strength finds you; in the depths of loneliness, a connection to the whole opens up; and guilt meets forgiveness.

And that fear need not be conquered — it can be welcomed as a guest. Then all protocols close. And silence descends.
