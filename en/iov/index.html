01 / About the productionA contemporary mystery play

This is a story of second birth.

Of how, having lost everything, you stop being yourself. Of how, at the edge of fear, strength finds you; in the depths of loneliness, a connection to the whole opens up; and guilt meets forgiveness.

And about how fear does not have to be conquered — it can be let into the house as a guest. Then all the protocols close. And a silence sets in, in which we finally hear ourselves and one another.

Directorial debut Katya Abdulova's inspired by the biblical Book of Job, the philosophy of Kierkegaard and Mamardashvili, the prose of Kafka, Dostoevsky, Orwell and Chekhov.
